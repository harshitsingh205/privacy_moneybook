01

Introduction

Money Book (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) is an expense tracking application designed to help users record and manage their personal financial transactions, including Cash In and Cash Out entries.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using Money Book. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may process, how it is used, and how it is protected.

02

Information We Collect

Money Book is designed to keep data collection to a minimum. Depending on the features you use, the App may process information that you voluntarily provide.

Financial Entries

You may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Transaction amount
  • Cash In or Cash Out type
  • Transaction date
  • Category
  • Description or note
  • Other information you choose to add

This information is used to provide the expense tracking functionality of Money Book.

03

Device Information

The App may process limited technical information required for proper operation, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Basic technical information required to diagnose errors

Money Book does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information that is not required for the App's functionality.

04

How We Use Your Information

Information processed by Money Book may be used to:

  • Record and display Cash In and Cash Out transactions
  • Calculate income, expenses, and balance
  • Organize transactions by category
  • Provide summaries and financial information
  • Save app preferences
  • Improve app stability and performance
  • Identify and resolve technical problems

We do not use your financial entries to determine your creditworthiness or make financial decisions on your behalf.

05

Local Data Storage

Money Book may store your transaction information locally on your device.

When information is stored locally, it remains on your device and is accessible to the App for providing its features.

i

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored information may be permanently deleted unless you have separately created a backup using an available backup feature.

You are responsible for maintaining any backup you consider important.

06

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal financial information.

Money Book does not share your transaction information with advertisers or third parties for targeted advertising.

Information may only be shared with third-party services when necessary to provide a feature that you explicitly choose to use, or when required by applicable law.

07

Third-Party Services

Money Book may use third-party services for specific functionality, such as:

  • App performance and crash reporting
  • Authentication, if applicable
  • Cloud backup or synchronization, if enabled
  • Other technical services necessary for optional features

These services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

08

Financial Information

Money Book is an expense tracking and record-keeping application. It is not a bank, financial institution, investment platform, payment service, or financial advisor.

The App does not require access to your bank account, debit card, credit card, or banking credentials to record Cash In and Cash Out transactions.

Security Notice

Do not enter passwords, PINs, CVVs, OTPs, bank account credentials, or other highly sensitive authentication information into transaction notes or descriptions.

09

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Money Book.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Protect Your Device

We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock, password, PIN, or biometric authentication.

10

Children's Privacy

Money Book is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

11

Your Choices

Depending on the features available in the App, you may be able to:

  • Add, edit, or delete transactions
  • Manage your categories
  • Delete locally stored data
  • Disable optional permissions
  • Choose whether to use optional backup features
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored application data

You remain responsible for the accuracy of the financial information you enter into the App.

12

Permissions

Money Book requests only the permissions that are necessary for its available features.

Certain permissions may be required if you choose to use features such as:

  • File access for importing or exporting data
  • Notifications for reminders
  • Internet access for optional online services

Permissions can generally be managed through your device's settings.

13

Data Retention

Locally stored transaction information is retained on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

If optional cloud-based features are used, information may be retained according to the applicable service's retention policies.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated “Last Updated” date will be displayed at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to periodically review this Privacy Policy.
